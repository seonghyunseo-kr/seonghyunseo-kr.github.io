#### Problem
- Quality risks in aircraft manufacturing (e.g., delays and defects) need to be anticipated before they escalate, and the root causes of warning-level signals need to be identified.

#### My Role
- AI/ML Research Intern at Boeing AI (BKETC), working with global teammates and stakeholders.

#### Approach
- Early-warning forecasting of manufacturing quality risks.
- Causal root-cause analysis of warning-level signals.
- An automated reporting pipeline connecting forecasting and root-cause analysis to report generation.

#### Outcome
- Prototyped an end-to-end pipeline from forecasting to automated reporting, refined through iterative deployment with stakeholder feedback.
